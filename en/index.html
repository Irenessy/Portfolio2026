I start with how the work gets done.

Industrial design taught me to pay attention to how people use things. Consulting taught me to ask why a process works the way it does. I bring both perspectives to the workflows and interfaces I design.

I’m a Strategy Consultant & UI/UX Designer at IT Consultis. At NIO Energy, I worked on equipment monitoring, charging products, and internal tools. I studied interaction and experience design at Jiangnan University and industrial design at Shanghai University.
